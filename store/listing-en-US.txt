=== APP NAME (<= 30 chars) ===
Entrelares

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (<= 80 chars) ===
Shared custody calendar: who has the child today, and every day swap.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (<= 4000 chars) ===
Two homes, one childhood. Entrelares organizes life after a divorce: one shared calendar shows who has the child today, who takes over next, and the handoff time for each day.

WHO HAS THE CHILD TODAY
Open the app and the answer is on the first screen. The month calendar colors each caregiver's days, and shows the handoff time whenever the child changes homes.

SWAPS BOTH SIDES APPROVE
Need to trade a day? Propose the swap with a short message ("doctor's appointment at 2pm") — nothing moves on the calendar until the other caregiver agrees. Whoever answers can reply with a note, and all of it is recorded. If nobody answers, the request is auto-approved after 48 hours, with a reminder first.

A HISTORY NOBODY CAN REWRITE
Every calendar change is recorded: who asked, who approved, and the messages they exchanged. The history cannot be edited or deleted — by anyone. On the Premium plan, export a PDF report of any period with the full timeline.

PERIOD SUMMARY
See how many days each caregiver had — planned and actual —, how many swaps each one gave up and received, and the projected balance including the future swaps already agreed on.

MORE CAREGIVERS IN THE ROUTINE
Grandparents, a nanny or another caregiver can join the family with their own role, invited by e-mail by an administrator.

ALWAYS UP TO DATE
With the app open, the calendar updates in real time: requests, approvals and changes appear without a reload. With the app closed, every request, approval and deadline reminder arrives by e-mail.

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USE
• In English and Portuguese — each person in the family reads in their own language, notifications and e-mails included
• Built for the phone
• The same calendar in the browser, at https://web.entrelares.app
